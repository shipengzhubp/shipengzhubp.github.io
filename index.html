Shipeng Zhu (朱 士鹏)

I am an Assistant Researcher (至善博后), at PALM Lab, School of Computer Science and Engineering, Southeast University. I received the Ph.D. degree from Southeast University, China, in 2025, advised by Prof. Hui Xue.

My research interests mainly include computer vision and multi-modal learning. I'm currently working on text image processing & analysis.
